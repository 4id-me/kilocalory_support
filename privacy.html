Privacy Policy

Kilocalory

Last updated: October 30, 2026

Overview

Kilocalory is designed with your privacy in mind. Your diary, weight logs and settings live on your iPhone. No sign-up, no email, no name, no account. Nothing you log leaves the phone.

Data Collection

The app does not:

• Ask for your name, email or any personal information
• Track your usage or behavior
• Use analytics, advertising or tracking services
• Use cookies or similar technologies
• Sell or share data with third parties for their own purposes

Camera Access

The app uses the camera to scan product barcodes and nutrition labels. Scanning is processed in real time on the device; the camera image is not stored or transmitted.

Network Requests

When you scan a barcode or search for a product, the app sends the barcode number or the search text to a food database API to retrieve nutrition information. Only the barcode number or the search text is sent — no personal data, device identifiers, or location information is included in these requests.

On-Device Storage

All your food entries, weight logs, and settings are stored locally on your iPhone. If you delete the app, that data is permanently removed. You can export a backup file yourself from the Backup & Restore screen.

Children's Privacy

The app does not collect any data from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

If we update this privacy policy, we will post the changes on this page with an updated date.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please open an issue on GitHub.
