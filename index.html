Kilocalory

Track calories and macros — fast, private, no account needed.

About

Kilocalory is a simple calorie and macro tracker for iOS. Scan barcodes, log meals manually, and see your daily protein, fat, and carbs breakdown. All data stays on your device.

Features

  • Barcode scanning — instantly look up nutrition data from millions of products
  • Manual logging — add meals with calories, weight, and macros
  • Macro tracking — see protein, fat, and carbs in grams, calories, and percentages
  • Color-coded protein — visual indicator helps you hit your daily targets
  • Smart estimates — projected daily total based on your eating pattern
  • Quick duplicate — repeat frequent meals with one tap
  • Body tracking — log weight and waist circumference over time
  • Custom targets — set your own macro percentage goals
  • 100% on-device — no account, no cloud, no tracking

How to Use

  • Tap + to add a meal — scan a barcode or enter manually
  • View your daily totals and macro breakdown on the main screen
  • Swipe between days to review past entries
  • Tap the menu icon to access weight tracking and macro target settings

FAQ

Is my data safe?

Yes. All data is stored locally on your iPhone. Nothing is uploaded to any server. The app does not collect any personal information.

Why does the app need camera access?

The camera is used only to scan product barcodes. No photos are taken or stored.

What do the macro percentages mean?

Percentages show the share of total calories from each macronutrient. Protein and carbs provide 4 kcal per gram, fat provides 9 kcal per gram. This is the standard way to express macronutrient ratios in nutrition.

What do the protein colors mean?

Green means you're meeting your protein target, yellow means you're below but close, and red means you're significantly below. You can customize these thresholds in settings.
